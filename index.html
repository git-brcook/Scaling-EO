The Scaling Framework

Operate from the future, not the past.

The past is feedback; the present is where work happens; but only the future can organize both. The framework rests on three operating moves — apply them in order and the rest follows.

01 / Frame
Set the future.
An impossible goal — 10x in 3 years, not 10–20 — does the strategic work. When the frame is high enough, only one or two paths survive. Position validates itself through the goal; analysis doesn't.
02 / Floor
Raise the line.
Define the standard below which you refuse to operate. Most businesses are below their floor in 80% of activity. Complexity is how power-law operators get sucked back into the bell curve.
03 / Focus
One axis. All weight.
Concentrate on the few pathways and compounding assets that produce power-law returns. Innovation is saying no to a thousand good things to commit to the one that compounds.

Level 01 — Strategy

Be different,
not better.

Strategy is escape, not victory. Competition is for losers — the goal is to occupy an axis where no one else is competing in the first place.

Principle 01
The impossible goal does the strategic work.
When the frame is high enough, only one or two paths survive. Position validates itself through the goal — analysis doesn't.
Principle 02
Own an axis no one else competes on.
Better is bell-curve work. Different is power-law work. The whole game is finding — and committing to — a non-substitutable position.
Principle 03
The 76% rule.
Category leaders capture roughly three-quarters of the value. There is no prize for second place in a power-law market.
Level 02 — System

Raise the floor.

Most businesses operate below their floor in 80% of activity. Every task you keep that lives below the line is a force quietly pulling you back to the mean.

The amateur
Tries to do too much.

Spreads attention across every activity, every opportunity, every audience — and ends up running every system below its floor.

The professional
Refuses below the floor.

Cuts anything below the 99.99th percentile of the axis. Knows the cost of complexity. Defends the line ruthlessly.

Level 03 — Scale

Compound the moat.

Depth is the moat. Most stop at competence — mastery is the willingness to go deeper for years past it. Once depth, distribution, and IP feed each other, the flywheel runs on its own.

10x Vision Workshop

Operate & organize
from the scale, not the climb.

The future doesn't arrive — you reverse-engineer back from it. Six questions to translate your power-law axis into a 3-year, fully-specified result.

01
What does literal 10x look like for you — not in 10–20 years, but 3?
02
What would the 10x result be?
03
If you operated completely from that 10x result, how would that change what you're doing now?
04
What would the 10x model be? How many units, at what price, to whom?
05
What — and who — would fall below the floor, that you're currently doing?
06
Who would you need to involve? What are the power-law pathways to reach 10x in 3 years?
